### 💹 1. UltraTech Cement — Fundamental Equity Valuation & DCF Analysis

*An end-to-end fundamental valuation model combining financial statement analysis, peer analysis, regression-based beta estimation, WACC, FCFF forecasting and DCF valuation.*

> 💡 **Key Points:** Built a complete fundamental valuation model for UltraTech Cement using historical financial data, financial ratios, peer comparison, 5-year weekly beta regression against NIFTY 50, unlevered/relevered beta, CAPM, capital structure, WACC, FCFF forecasting and DCF valuation. The model estimated an intrinsic value of approximately **₹7,383 per share** under the base-case assumptions.

* **The Analytical Challenge:** Connected financial statement analysis with quantitative market-risk estimation. Python and `yfinance` were used to acquire and structure the 5-year weekly market data required for beta regression, which was then incorporated into the CAPM, WACC and DCF workflow.

  * [💻 Review Financial Model, Valuation & Files](https://github.com/Shubhyam-f/UltraTech-Cement-Fundamental-Equity-Valuation-DCF-Analysis)

### 🍕 3. Pizza Place Operations & Sales Analysis

*A relational Power BI model analysing restaurant operations, product performance and sales behaviour.*

> 💡 **Executive Business Insight:** Identified that while the **Classic** pizza category generated the highest overall order volume, the **Supreme** category produced a 14% higher average profit margin per unit. The analysis highlighted an opportunity to evaluate promotional focus around higher-margin products during peak periods.

* **The Data Modelling Challenge:** Normalized a multi-table dataset containing `orders`, `order_details`, `pizzas` and `pizza_types` into a functioning **Star Schema**, allowing item-level analysis without duplicating revenue and order totals.

  * [💻 Review Data Architecture & Files](https://github.com/Shubhyam-f/Pizza-Sales-Performance-Analysis)
