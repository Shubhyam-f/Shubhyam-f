**Focus:** Fundamental Analysis | Segment-Based Forecasting | FCFF Valuation

A comprehensive equity research project involving business analysis, segment-wise revenue forecasting, financial modelling, and discounted cash flow valuation.

**Key work:**
- Analysed Titan's business segments and historical financial performance.
- Developed bottom-up revenue forecasts across major business segments.

- Explicit forecast period: 5 years
